## 📌 What is Pak Sender? — What does this project do?

Pak Sender is one platform for all business messaging in Pakistan and worldwide: bulk & OTP SMS, voice OTP calls, email marketing campaigns, branded invoices & receipts, recurring invoices, daily payment reports and customer automations (thank-you, loyalty points, offers) — sent by hand from the website or automatically from your own software through one REST API key. Email Marketing (email.paksender.com) is part of Pak Sender itself.

## 🎯 Who is it for?

Shops & e-commerce, schools, ISPs, gyms, clinics, distributors, landlords and developers in Pakistan and worldwide who need SMS, voice and email communication with their customers.

## ⭐ Key Features & Services — What it offers

- ✅ Bulk & OTP SMS with sender ID, Urdu (Unicode) support, delivery reports & CSV import — 99%+ delivery, 150+ countries
- ✅ Voice OTP & voice calls — code read aloud when SMS is slow (Rs 5/call)
- ✅ Email Marketing — campaigns, lists, templates, open & click tracking via your own SMTP (Rs 0.30/email)
- ✅ Invoices & receipts generated and sent by SMS or email in seconds
- ✅ Recurring invoices (daily / weekly / monthly) and daily reports & customer statements
- ✅ Customer automations: thank-you + loyalty points, shipped, review requests, sale & new-stock alerts
- ✅ WhatsApp Business: official Cloud API, shared inbox, keyword bot, AI assistant, campaigns
- ✅ REST API & signed webhooks — one API key, works with PHP, Laravel, WooCommerce, Node.js, Python,.NET
- ✅ Pay per message from one PKR balance — JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank or card; no monthly fee
